# Content authoring request for quote — unsent draft

Status: preparation only. No supplier has been contacted and no quote received.

Stellara seeks original, independently reviewed Common Entrance preparation
content for Nigerian Primary 5–6 learners. The draft curriculum has approximately
74 topics in five learning areas. Official 2027 exam mappings remain unverified;
the academic lead will approve scope before production is commissioned.

## Requested scope

- Approximately 1,800–2,200 items across practice, placement and reserved test pools.
- About 74 lessons, each containing 3–6 short cards and taking 5–8 minutes.
- Three full practice-test forms from a reserved pool, after blueprint approval.
- Placement material targeting 20 items per configured learning area, subject to
  academic approval of strand balance and final exam mapping.
- Worked explanations, hints, misconception-based distractors, difficulty and
  application tags, provenance, licensed-source evidence and accessible media.
- Delivery as schema-valid, versioned JSON and editable original media, with
  independent answer verification and an agreed correction process.

Planning estimate: 3,000–6,000 reviewed-content hours. This is a budget-planning
range from the project specification, not a supplier commitment or agreed price.

## Ask each supplier to provide

- Item and lesson unit prices, total price, currency, taxes and exclusions.
- Authoring and independent-review hours, team roles and relevant experience.
- A paid sample/pilot proposal, delivery milestones and capacity assumptions.
- Included revision rounds, correction turnaround and acceptance criteria.
- Approach to original work, rights evidence, independent review and accessible
  Nigerian English content; readiness to use counsel-approved agreements.
- Dependencies, payment milestones, quote validity and schedule risks.

Acceptance requires academic approval and independent answer verification, plus
successful schema checks and the separate rendering/media/readability checks when
those systems are implemented. Schema validity alone is not acceptance.
