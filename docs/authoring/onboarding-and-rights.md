# Author and reviewer onboarding

Status: preparation only. No contributor has been contracted or approved by this document.

Before the first item is accepted, the founder records that counsel-approved IP
assignment and originality warranties have been signed by each author and reviewer.
The academic lead confirms subject expertise, availability and independent review.

## Counsel-review brief

Ask counsel to prepare or approve the agreements for the actual engagement and
jurisdiction. Provide the intended deliverables, parties, working arrangements,
payment schedule, use of source material and intended product distribution.
Ask counsel to address ownership and assignment of commissioned work, permitted
third-party material and licences, originality warranties, reviewer obligations,
confidentiality, correction requests, disputes and termination. This checklist is
not an agreement and does not establish legal validity or transfer any rights.

## Onboarding checks

- Confirm the contributor's role, subjects and assigned work privately.
- Obtain counsel-approved signed agreements before accepting the first item.
- Explain original-work requirements: no copied past papers or exam-body logos.
- Record permitted licensed/public-domain sources and evidence references.
- Train authors on item schemas, Nigerian English, accessibility and explanations.
- Assign an independent reviewer; author and reviewer must be different people.
- Require verified answers, provenance, licence references where needed, image alt
  text and a recorded publish decision. Technical schema validation is only one gate.
- Explain correction and takedown escalation, and who can approve publication.
