13 · Family launch and operations

Stellara is sold directly to families, like a home tutor on the family phone (decided 17 Nov 2026). There are no schools, classes or licences. This document replaces the earlier school rollout plan.

1. Commercial model (to decide)

Item Proposal Status
Buyer The parent or guardian Agreed direction
Unit Per family per month or per term, Primary 5–6 for the MVP Proposal
Price Test 2–3 price points with beta families Open (Q2 in 01)
Free trial 14 days, no card needed to start Proposal
Siblings A second child at a lower price Proposal; Phase 2
Payment Paystack (card, bank transfer, USSD), built after M2 Proposal

2. The family beta (target: 50–100 families, from 1 Feb 2027)

Step What happens Owner
Recruit Parent WhatsApp groups, referrals from early families, a simple sign-up page Founder
Sign up Parent signs in (Google or email link), confirms 18+ Parent
Consent Plain-words notice, purposes as separate toggles, the approved verification check Parent
Add a child First name, school year, goal (for example Common Entrance and its date), picture key Parent
First week A welcome message, then the first weekly report on Sunday Stellara
Check-in A short call or WhatsApp survey with each family in week 2 and week 6 Family success

Materials: a one-page "How Stellara works" for parents (English first, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo later), a consent explainer and a short video of a child using the app (with the family's written permission). None of them use exam-body logos.

3. Parent rhythm

  • Daily: the child does today's set (about 10 minutes). The parent does nothing unless they want to.
  • Weekly: the parent reads the weekly report and, if they like, the suggested next step.
  • Monthly: for Primary 6, the parent can suggest a practice test at the weekend.
